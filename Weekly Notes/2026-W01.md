# Week 01 - 2026

**Week:** December 29, 2025 - January 4, 2026

## Overview

First week of Compiler Study focusing on foundational concepts: understanding compiler phases, the transformation from text to structure, and the assignment of meaning to syntactic structures.

## Daily Plans

### Thursday, January 1, 2026 — Day 01
**Topic:** What is a Compiler?
- **Focus:** [[01-foundations/day-01-what-is-a-compiler]]
- **Goals:**
  - Understand why compiler phases exist
  - Learn the six unavoidable phases: lexing, parsing, semantic analysis, IR, optimization, code generation
  - Recognize that each phase removes a specific ambiguity
- **Duration:** 60 minutes
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Can describe why phases exist without memorizing names
  - Understand that small languages don't eliminate phases, only shrink their scope
  - Feel oriented rather than overwhelmed

### Friday, January 2, 2026 — Day 02
**Topic:** From Text to Structure
- **Focus:** [[01-foundations/day-02-from-text-to-structure]]
- **Goals:**
  - Build concrete mental model of lexing and parsing
  - Understand the boundary: lexing creates chunks (tokens), parsing creates shape (trees)
  - Work through example: `x = 3 + 4 * 5` from text → tokens → AST
- **Duration:** 60 minutes
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Can describe inputs, outputs, and failure modes of lexing vs parsing
  - Understand that structure is a form of disambiguation
  - Recognize that precedence is encoded in tree shape

### Saturday, January 3, 2026 — Day 03
**Topic:** From Structure to Meaning
- **Focus:** [[01-foundations/day-03-structure-to-meaning]]
- **Goals:**
  - Understand semantic analysis: assigning meaning to valid syntax
  - Learn three classes of semantic checks: name resolution, type checking, contextual constraints
  - Distinguish between syntactic errors (parse fails) and semantic errors (parse succeeds but meaning is invalid)
- **Duration:** 60 minutes
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Can list three things semantic analysis checks
  - Understand symbol tables and type environments
  - Recognize that valid parse tree ≠ valid program

### Sunday, January 4, 2026 — Day 04
**Topic:** From Meaning to Representation
- **Focus:** [[01-foundations/day-04-meaning-to-representation]]
- **Goals:**
  - Understand why AST is insufficient for optimization and code generation
  - Learn what Intermediate Representation (IR) is and why it exists
  - Contrast AST (source-oriented) with IR (machine-oriented)
  - Explore how IR makes control flow and data flow explicit
- **Duration:** 60 minutes
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Can explain why we need IR in addition to AST
  - Understand the difference between three-address code and SSA
  - Recognize that IR enables transformation while AST enables validation
