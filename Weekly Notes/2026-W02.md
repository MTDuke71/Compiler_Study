# Week 02 - 2026

**Week:** January 5 - January 11, 2026

## Overview

Second week of Compiler Study focusing on invariants and constraints: understanding what stays constant across transformations, what constraints limit design choices, and how representations force tradeoffs. This week deepens foundational understanding and completes the high-level overview of all six compiler phases before diving into implementation.

## Daily Plans

### Monday, January 5, 2026 — Day 05
**Topic:** Ambiguity and Phases (Review/Deepen)
- **Focus:** [[01-foundations/ambiguity-and-phases]]
- **Goals:**
  - Review how each phase removes specific ambiguities
  - Deepen understanding of phase boundaries and dependencies
  - Identify what information is preserved vs. transformed at each phase
  - Understand why phases cannot be arbitrarily reordered
- **Duration:** 60 minutes
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Can explain what each phase preserves (invariants)
  - Understand the "contract" between adjacent phases
  - Recognize that phase order is determined by information dependencies

### Tuesday, January 6, 2026 — Day 06
**Topic:** Language as State
- **Focus:** [[01-foundations/language-as-state]]
- **Goals:**
  - Understand compilation as state transformation
  - Learn how each phase manages and transforms program state
  - Recognize state invariants that must hold throughout compilation
  - See how control flow and data flow relate to state
- **Duration:** 60 minutes
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Can describe compilation as a series of state transformations
  - Understand what state means at each phase (tokens, AST, IR, etc.)
  - Recognize that optimizations are state-preserving transformations

### Wednesday, January 7, 2026 — Day 07
**Topic:** Invariants
- **Focus:** [[zettel/Z0004-invariants]] and [[00-index/invariants]]
- **Goals:**
  - Learn what invariants are and why they matter
  - Identify key invariants in each compiler phase
  - Understand how invariants enable safe transformations
  - Document invariants that must hold in your own implementations
- **Duration:** 60 minutes
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Can list 3-5 critical compiler invariants
  - Understand how to verify invariants are maintained
  - Recognize when a proposed optimization would violate an invariant

### Thursday, January 8, 2026 — Day 08
**Topic:** Design Tradeoffs
- **Focus:** Constraints and tradeoffs in compiler design
- **Goals:**
  - Understand common tradeoffs: speed vs. memory, simplicity vs. generality
  - Recognize constraints that limit design choices
  - See how different representations optimize for different goals
  - Document personal "aha" moments and begin "common misconceptions" list
- **Duration:** 60 minutes
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Can identify tradeoffs in at least 3 design decisions
  - Understand why no representation is universally optimal
  - Started a "common misconceptions" list

### Friday, January 9, 2026 — Day 09
**Topic:** From Representation to Optimization
- **Focus:** Overview of optimization and code generation
- **Goals:**
  - Understand how IR enables optimization
  - Learn what code generation does and why it's the final phase
  - Complete the high-level mental model of all six phases
  - Prepare for implementation work starting Week 3
- **Duration:** 60 minutes
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Can explain why optimization needs IR
  - Understand what code generation produces (machine code/bytecode)
  - Have complete mental map of all six phases
  - Ready to begin lexer implementation

### Weekend, January 10-11, 2026
**Optional Activities:**
- Review week's notes and consolidate understanding
- Sketch pseudocode for a minimal lexer (design only, no code)
- Expand [[zettel/Z0004-invariants]] with insights from the week
- Prepare questions for Week 3 (lexing deep dive)
- Read ahead: [[02-lexing/README]]
